Set one in LockedIn

  1. Download the Mac version on your Mac and the iPhone version on your iPhone. On iPhone it lands in the Files app, in Downloads.
  2. In LockedIn, open Settings, then Appearance, and choose Own Photo.
  3. Pick the wallpaper from Photos or Files. Leave Same wallpaper on all devices off, so each device keeps its own version.
