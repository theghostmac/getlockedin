Set one in LockedIn

  1. Download the Mac or iPhone version above. On iPhone, tap and hold the image and choose Save to Photos.
  2. In LockedIn, open Settings, then Appearance, and choose Own Photo.
  3. Pick the wallpaper from Photos or Files. Turn on Same wallpaper on all devices if you want it everywhere.
