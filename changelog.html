RiTA, Reading and quick sessions

MaciPhone1.0 (2)

Meet RiTA, LockedIn's assistant on Apple Intelligence, and a new place for everything you mean to read.

  • RiTAWrite your standup, ask what to work on next, or turn notes into tasks. Find it in Today's Brief and in ⌘K. It runs on your device, so nothing about your work leaves it.
  • ReadingA new tab for saved articles. Paste a link and LockedIn keeps the text, then opens it in a calm reader with your place saved across devices.
  • SummariesRiTA boils any saved article down to its key points.
  • Quick sessions, redoneStart one right from the Focus card or ⌘K. It gets its own timer and tasks, and Make main swaps it with your main session.
  • Connect GitHub in one stepNo setup needed: open Settings, then Integrations, and tap Connect GitHub.
  • FixedThe New entry button no longer covers Pause and End on iPhone, and Today's Brief no longer cuts off your focus time.

The public beta

MaciPhone1.0 (1)

LockedIn is on TestFlight for Mac and iPhone. One link installs both, and Sign in with Apple keeps them in sync.

  • Install from TestFlightOpen the beta link on your Mac or iPhone and TestFlight handles the rest. New builds arrive on their own.
  • Matching themesA set of wallpapers in Mac and iPhone pairs, so both screens look the same. Find them on the Themes page.
  • Built for the App StoreThe Mac app now runs in Apple's sandbox and keeps its data in its own container.
  • FixedA crash on first launch while setting up the calendar during onboarding.
October 27, 2026

Focus, rebuilt

MaciPhone

Ending a session no longer asks you for anything. Home was rebuilt around what you are doing now.

  • One tap to endThe tasks you finished during a session become its summary. End it and read what you did, with nothing to type.
  • Resume within 30 minutesEnded a session by mistake? Pick it back up from the summary and the time in between counts as a pause.
  • Focus takes the stageThe running session is now the biggest thing on Home, with a ring that fills each hour and the task list beside it.
  • Now, Next, DoneHome reads top to bottom as your current session, what is coming up, and what you finished today.
  • Command barPress ⌘K to add a task, switch project, pause, end, or jump anywhere without the mouse.
  • Evening wrap upFrom 5 PM Home shows your day at a glance, with a quick way to plan tomorrow and share the day.
  • Edit in placeTap a task to rename it right there. Return saves, Escape cancels. No more pencil icons.
  • New typeNewsreader for headlines and Manrope for everything else, on one consistent scale.
October 6, 2026

Rest days and a one minute setup

MaciPhone

Streaks now respect the days you plan to rest.

  • Choose your rest daysPick Saturday, Sunday or both. Resting on them never breaks your streak.
  • A fairer streakToday only counts against you once it is over, so a slow morning never shows a broken streak.
  • Faster onboardingSetup takes about a minute: your first project, rest days, calendar and notifications.

Your day, everywhere

MaciPhone

Meetings join your tasks on Home, and LockedIn reaches your Home Screen.

  • Today's Brief on your Lock ScreenOn iPhone, the brief arrives as a notification each morning.
  • The shape of the dayHome shows today's meetings as blocks, with the free time between them where focus fits.
  • WidgetsYour Day and Today's Page widgets for the Home Screen, refreshed as you work.
  • GitHub in the briefConnect GitHub to see reviews waiting on you and what is blocking your pull requests. Add any of them to today.
  • Projects, redesignedEach project now leads with its recent activity, hours and progress.
